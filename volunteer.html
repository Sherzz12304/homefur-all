Find a Shelter

Volunteer

Give your time, change a life.

Shelters run on people who show up. Whether it's an hour a week or a Saturday a month, your time directly means more animals get fed, walked, and loved on their way to a home.

Ways to volunteer

Pick what fits your schedule and skills — every role below is needed right now across our pawtner shelters.

Shelter care

Walking, feeding, cleaning, and giving animals daily attention on-site.

Events & fundraising

Help run adoption drives, donation booths, and community fundraisers.

Foster-to-adopt support

Support foster families with supplies, advice, or short-term relief care.

Admin & social media

Help write pet bios, manage photos, or reply to adoption inquiries.

Transport

Drive animals to vet visits, foster homes, or between partner shelters.

Vet-day helper

Assist during vaccination and check-up days at partner clinics.

Before you sign up

A few basics so we can match you with the right role.

Age

16 years or older

Under 18 volunteers need a guardian's signed consent.

Time

2 hours minimum

Most roles ask for at least 2 hours, twice a month.

A short onboarding walkthrough before your first shift.
